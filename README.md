## ✨ Features

- ✅ **Fully Responsive** — Mobile, tablet, desktop sab pe perfect
- ✅ **Scroll Progress Bar** — Top pe reading progress
- ✅ **Smooth Scrolling** — Nav links pe click karne pe smooth jump
- ✅ **Reveal Animations** — Elements scroll pe fade-in hote hain
- ✅ **Working Contact Form** — Formspree ke through (free)
- ✅ **Back to Top Button** — Scroll karne pe show hota hai
- ✅ **Mobile Hamburger Menu** — Chhoti screen pe menu
- ✅ **SEO Meta Tags** — Google search ke liye
- ✅ **Accessibility** — `prefers-reduced-motion` support
- ✅ **No Framework** — Pure HTML/CSS/JS (koi React/Bootstrap nahi)
- ✅ **Fast Load** — No build step needed
